# Reconstruction method

1. Curate and licence-check references.
2. Establish coordinate convention and scale anchors.
3. Fit a coarse footprint/massing model.
4. Solve or approximate selected modern camera viewpoints.
5. Compare silhouettes and architectural landmarks.
6. Refine major masses: transept, triconch, westwork, towers, roofs.
7. Add repeated buttresses/openings parametrically.
8. Add tracery/details only after coarse validation converges.
9. Record every inferred dimension.
10. Produce validation renders and an uncertainty report per iteration.

Classical SfM/photogrammetry may be used as a geometric aid, but the final model should remain auditable and structurally intelligible rather than only a dense anonymous triangle mesh.
